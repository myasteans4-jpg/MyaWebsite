What is Drawing?

Drawing is a creative process of marking marking, lines and shapes on a flat surface to create a picture or a design. You can use pencils, pens, crayons and pastels. You can draw anything you want, just be creative and make your own art!

Types of Drawings

Sketch

Sketch drawing is a fast, rough, freehand drawing that caputures the main idea or shape of a subject without using fine details. To sketch, get a pencil and sketchbook or paper, then draw lines, circles and loops on the paper, you can also sketch simple shapes to map out your subject. Sketching uses many shapes, sizes and styles. You can sketch any way you want.

Anime

Anime drawing is the creation of characters and scenes using distinct, expressive visual style. It is also a distinct art style based on Japanese animation and comics. Anime drawing is a type of cartoon drawing, there both use to tell stories and express creativity. You can make your anime drawing any way you want, be creative on your art.

Architectural

Architectural drawing is a detailed technical plan or visual guide used to design, and construct a building or structure. Construction workers, engineers and architectures use architectural drawings as a step by step plan to see what to build and where everything goes, like a blueprint. Using an architectural drawing is very helpful because they turn loose thoughs into clear plans that guide a project from the first idea to the final build.

Line

Line drawing is an artwork made using only distinct straight or curved lines against a plain background without any solid shading or color gradients. It focus on using distinct marks and strokes to define edges, shapes and forms. There are many types of techniques for line drawing, like outline, contour drawing and cross contour. When line drawing, you can design any art you want.
