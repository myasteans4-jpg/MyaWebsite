What is Dancing?

Dancing is the act of moving your body to music or a rhythm to express your feelings or tell a story. Dancing is also a great way to exercise that provides strong, mental benefits. You can dance by yourself, with your family, friends or anyone. People dance for celebrating birthdays, graduation, weddings, holidays and more. There are many kinds of dances around the world and everybody loves to dance!
