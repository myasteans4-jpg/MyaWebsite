What is Dancing?

Dancing is the act of moving your body to music or a rhythm to express your feelings or tell a story. Dancing is also a great way to exercise that provides strong, mental benefits. You can dance by yourself, with your family, friends or anyone. People dance for celebrating birthdays, graduation, weddings, holidays and more. There are many kinds of dances around the world and everybody loves to dance!

Types of Dancing

Ballet

Ballet dancing is a sttuctured and graceful dance form that combines strict body control, precise steps andclassical music to tell a story or express an emotion. The technique is a built on strict posture and balance (rotating the legs outward from the hips). You can see ballet dancing at a theaters or on TV with your friends and family. Ballet dancing is a worderful because it's a way to xperience and enjoy the art of ballet.

Tap

Tap dancing is the syle of dance where your feet act as musical instruments, making crisp rhythmic sounds by tapping the floor with metal-plated shoes. Every step creates a percussive sound. You have to move your body like a dancer while keeping time lie a drummer, try to keep up the rhythm. Tap dancing started in the U.S in the early century through the blending of African and British dance traditions. There is a lot of tap dancing in broadway, it has been featured in many classic and modern musicals.

Tango

Tango dancing is an inimate and partner dance built on a silent, physical conversation between two people. To tango, partner with someone, stand up straight with your shoulders back and head high. blend your knees slightly, stand about six inches apart from your partner in a closed position, place your right hand on your partner's back then hold your other hands at shoulder height. Tango dancing is a great way to be together and working together to dance.

Salsa

Salsa dancing is a lively and energetic social partner dance set to upbeat Latin Americam music. To salsa, dancers take three steps for every four beats of music, pausing on the fourth beat. For the partner, one person leads and the other follows, moving through spins and turns. Working together makes it better to salsa dance.
