What is Cooking?

Cooking is the process to preparing ingredients into delicious food by using heat. Heading creates new taste and smells to make food delicious. A tool people can use to cook is a pot, pan or skillet. They can also use a oven or stove to cook their food. Cooking is a great way to make yummy food, especially getting together with your friends and family to enjoy it.

Types of Cooking

Frying

Frying is where food is cooked in hot oil or fat to cook food fast. Frying works by putting food into a pan or pot filled with hot oil, chooose the right oil like canola or peanut oil, cook the food between 350 degrees Fahrenheit and 375 degrees Fahrenheit, prepare the food by adding sides to your frying course. There are other types of frying like deep frying, pan frying and stir-frying.

Steaming

Steaming is a gentle method that uses water vapor to cook food without submerging it in liquid. Steaming works by using boiling water where a pot of water heats until it turns into steam, heat transfer where steam rises, wraps around the food and condenses, transferring heat to cook the item.

Broiling

Broiling is a fast cooking method that uses very high direct heat from an overheated element in a oven. Broiling is like a upside down grilling, the heat comes from the top coils or burner instead of the bottom. The food sits very close to the heat source like 3 to 5 inches away.

Grilling

Grilling is a fast cooking method that uses direct, high heat from an open flame or hot surface to cook food quickly. Grilling works by cooking food on metal grates oropen bars over a heat source, set the grill at 300 degrees Fahrenheit to over 500 degrees Fahrenheit. Most meals finish in mintutes rather than hours.
